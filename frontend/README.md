## 🚀 Overview

ASTERIA is an AI-powered Business Research Workspace designed for business students, consulting competitions, academic research and strategic analysis.

Instead of acting like a chatbot, ASTERIA provides a complete research workflow:

- Research Planning
- Evidence Collection
- AI-powered Analysis
- Consulting-style Report Generation
- Persistent Project Workspace

Our goal is to build the operating system for business research.

### Project Workspace
